## Seven card stud

Before Hold'em took over, **seven card stud** was the most popular poker game. It has no community cards.

**The deal.**

1. Everyone antes. Each player gets two cards face down and one face up.
2. The lowest up card must post a small forced bet, the **bring-in**. Betting follows.
3. Three more cards are dealt face up, one at a time, each with a round of betting.
4. The last card is dealt face down, with a final round of betting.

Each player has seven cards, three hidden and four visible. You make the best five-card hand from your seven, exactly as in Hold'em, so the same hand ranking applies.

Stud is usually played with **fixed limits**: bets come in set sizes, smaller on early streets and doubled on later ones. Big bluffs matter less and careful odds matter more.

## Dead cards

The key stud skill is reading the up cards. Any card you can see in another player's hand is **dead**: it cannot come to you.

If you hold four hearts and see three hearts showing around the table, your flush draw has only 6 hearts left instead of 9. The same goes for pairs: if both remaining kings are showing elsewhere, you cannot make trip kings.

This is card removal from the combinatorics lesson, but you get to see far more cards.

## Razz

**Razz** is seven card stud played for **low**. The lowest five-card hand wins.

- Aces are low.
- Straights and flushes do not count, so A-2-3-4-5 is the best possible hand (the wheel).
- Pairs are bad. You want five different low cards.
- Hands are compared from the highest card down: 7-5-4-3-2 beats 8-4-3-2-A, because seven is lower than eight.

In razz the **highest** up card brings it in, and the best low showing acts first on later streets. Starting hands with three low, different cards, such as A-2-4, are strong. A high card or a pair in the first three is usually a fold.

<Remember>

- Stud: no community cards, seven cards each, three down and four up
- Best five of seven, normal rankings
- The bring-in replaces the blinds. Fixed limits are usual
- Visible cards are dead. Count them before counting outs
- Razz: lowest hand wins, aces low, straights and flushes ignored

</Remember>

<Mistakes>

- Counting outs without removing up cards
- Chasing draws whose cards are already showing
- In razz, keeping a pair or a high card because it looks strong
- Forgetting razz reverses the bring-in

</Mistakes>
